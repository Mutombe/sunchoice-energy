F.A.Q.

Good to know.

Straight answers on solar and costs.

How often should panels be cleaned?

It depends on dust, birds and trees. Most Harare roofs need a clean every few months in the dry season. We will suggest a schedule.

Do you service systems you didn't install?

Yes. Our maintenance and cleaning service covers any solar system, whoever installed it, including a health check of panels, inverter and batteries.

How long do solar panels last?

Most panels carry 20 to 25 year performance warranties and keep generating long after that with regular cleaning.

Can I still use solar on cloudy days?

Yes. Panels capture diffuse light on cloudy or rainy days, at reduced output. Batteries store surplus from sunny days to cover the gaps.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on size and equipment. Try our sizing calculator, then request a free quotation.
