Our story

A Zimbabwean company built around local sun.

Sunchoice Energy is a Zimbabwean solar company. Homeowners and businesses come back to us because the systems we fit keep working.

We specialise in the design, installation and maintenance of solar energy systems, sized to how you actually use power, from a single home backup to a whole factory roof.
