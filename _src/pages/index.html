Ask a question
How does solar energy work?

Panels turn sunlight into DC electricity. An inverter changes it to the AC your home uses, and any surplus charges your batteries.

What are the benefits of installing solar panels?

Lower bills, less generator fuel and, with batteries, power through outages. A quality system also adds value to your property.

How long do solar panels last?

Most panels carry 20 to 25 year performance warranties, and with regular cleaning they keep generating well beyond that.

Does solar work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes. Panels still capture diffuse sunlight, though output is lower than on clear days. Surplus energy from sunny days can be stored in batteries to cover cloudy periods.

How much does an installation cost?

It depends on the size of the system and the equipment. Request a free quotation and we will give you an exact figure for your property.
